3:14 AM

Bed

The ceiling has a crack in it that only shows up at this hour, when the streetlight comes through the blinds at exactly the wrong angle. You have been staring at it for eleven minutes. You know this because you checked, and checking was the mistake, because now the number is in your head and numbers at 3 AM do not leave quietly.

Your body is tired. That is not in question. But something underneath the tired is awake and pacing, and it has been pacing since you closed your eyes, and it does not seem interested in your opinion on the matter.

You could stay here and negotiate with the ceiling a while longer. Or you could get up.

Get up and go to the kitchen.
You can't sleep. Go to the window instead.
