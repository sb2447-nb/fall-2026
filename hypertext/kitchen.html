Kitchen

The fridge light is too honest for this hour. It shows you everything — the leftover rice, the wilting cilantro, the jar of something you bought with intentions you no longer remember. You are not hungry. You are just standing here because standing here is a kind of doing something, and doing something feels better than lying in the dark being done to.

On top of the fridge, half-hidden behind a stack of mail, there's a shoebox you haven't opened in a while. You know exactly what's in it.

The tile is cold under your feet. You could go back to bed and pretend none of this happened. Or you could reach for the box.

Reach for the box of old photos.
Give up. Go back to bed.
