Drawer of Old Photos

The photos are curling slightly at the corners, the way old photos do when nobody has looked at them in years. Here is a summer you'd half-forgotten. Here is a face you used to know by heart and now have to work to remember, which feels like its own small betrayal.

There's a version of you in these photos who had no idea what was coming — good or bad — and it is strange to sit on cold tile at 3 AM and pity someone who was, by every account, having a perfectly nice day.

You find yourself wanting to tell someone about this feeling, even though there is nobody awake to tell. Or you could just put the box away and let the version of you in the photos keep having their nice day, undisturbed.

Text someone anyway.
Put the photos away. Go back to bed.
