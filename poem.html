Music in My Life

Music has always been an important part of my life. I started playing piano, violin, and flute when I was young. Playing these instruments helped me appreciate different kinds of music. I listen to music when I am happy, stressed, or just want to relax. Music can completely change my mood and help me express my feelings.

I also think music is interesting because everyone experiences it differently.
The same song can create different feelings and memories for different people. That is one of my favorite things about music.

Things I Like About Music

  • It can change my mood.
  • It brings back memories.
  • It connects people.

Instruments I Have Played

  1. Piano
  2. Violin
  3. Flute

I also enjoy finding new music online. I often use YouTube to discover new songs and artists.
